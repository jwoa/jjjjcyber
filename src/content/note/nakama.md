I needed a way to manage memberships that belong to a group and particularly how to update the endpoint and uniqe profiles of those individuals once the cards were created. So I decided to start building one.

https://jasona.online/nakama <-- will be online soon, in development

### Backstory

I have always had a love for unique experiences and activations. Things like secret cards and locations.

I wanted a dashboard and tool that I could easily use to manage NFC cards and their endpoints/redirections as well as the ability to manage users who are subscribed to a service. In the future I hope to use this tool with brands that specialize in loyalty and membersip programs.
